## Web Research Notes

### Prague / Czechia

- Prague Castle official opening hours: summer season is April 1 to October 31. Castle complex is 06:00-22:00; historical buildings are 09:00-17:00; St. Vitus Cathedral is Mon-Sat 09:00-17:00 and Sunday 12:00-17:00; South Tower is 10:00-18:00. Source: https://www.hrad.cz/en/prague-castle-for-visitors/opening-hours
- Old Town Hall / Astronomical Clock: Prague City Tourism says the apostles appear every hour between 08:00 and 23:00. April-December opening is 09:00-20:00. Source: https://www.prague.eu/en/object/places/3129/astronomical-clock
- Charles Bridge: Prague City Tourism describes it as Prague's oldest preserved bridge, completed in the early 1400s, with 30 statues and bridge towers. This supports early-morning or night-walk placement. Source: https://prague.eu/en/objevujte/charles-bridge-karluv-most/
- Powder Gate Tower: Prague City Tourism says June-September hours are 09:00-20:30, viewing gallery at 44 m, and the tower marks the start of the Royal Route. Source: https://prague.eu/en/objevujte/powder-gate-tower-prasna-brana/
- Cesky Krumlov Castle: the official castle page describes the complex as one of Central Europe's major historic sights, UNESCO-listed in 1992, with five courtyards and a seven-hectare castle park. Source: https://castle.ckrumlov.cz/en/zamek_oinf_sthrza/
- Cafe Savoy official hours: Mon-Fri 08:00-22:00, Sat-Sun and public holidays 09:00-22:00. Source: https://www.cafesavoy.ambi.cz/en/
- Cafe Imperial official hours: daily 07:00-23:00; reservation recommended as early as possible. Source: https://www.cafeimperial.cz/en/homepage/
